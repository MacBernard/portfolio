About

I'm Benedict Bernard Waziri, a software and web developer based in Gombe, Nigeria, and the founder of MacBernard Soft Solutions Limited — a company on a mission to promote digital adoption among small-scale businesses that want to operate digitally but can't afford complex software.

My day job is building. MacHub runs schools, MacSuite runs hotels and property businesses, MacPOS runs shops. Around the code, I manage web presence and social media for organizations, and I've spent seasons supporting health and humanitarian programs with data systems, registry updates and field coordination.

I hold a BSc in Computer Science from Gombe State University, and I care about the same thing on every project: clean interfaces, honest data, and software that keeps working after I leave.
